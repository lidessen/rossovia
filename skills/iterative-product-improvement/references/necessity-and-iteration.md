evidence handoff, not a task database or acceptance record.

## Compact necessity test

Admit `yes` only when the surface owns a distinct user decision, state, or
authority boundary and removal would make that task or recovery path worse.
Admit `no` only when the same user outcome is preserved by a canonical existing
surface with less unnecessary complexity. Admit `uncertain` when live evidence
cannot distinguish the two; record the smallest safe probe instead of arguing
from taste.

## Round handoff

The next round must cite the prior runtime/revision, observed result, changed
judgment, and disconfirming observation. If none of those changed, stop rather
than creating another cosmetic round. A worker return, screenshot, or passing
check remains evidence until the designated verifier or human owner admits the
result through the project's normal authority path.
