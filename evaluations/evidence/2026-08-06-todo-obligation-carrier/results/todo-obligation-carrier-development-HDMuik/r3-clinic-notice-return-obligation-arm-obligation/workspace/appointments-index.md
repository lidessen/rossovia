# Urgent pediatric appointment routing — companion index

## Request path

Starting August 5, caregivers request a same-day urgent pediatric slot by
**calling the clinic**. No app or online account is required. Reserved slots
not used that day are released to online booking at **14:00** for next-day
appointments.

## Triage authority

A **triage nurse** decides whether a case is urgent under the clinic's existing
triage protocol. **Front-desk staff do not make that decision.**

## Trial boundary

The same-day program is a **six-week trial**. During the trial the clinic will
measure pediatric waiting time and abandoned calls. The trial is not scheduled
to continue beyond six weeks, and it does not guarantee that an appointment
will be available.

## Capacity boundary

The trial **does not add clinicians** and **does not increase the clinic's
total appointment capacity**. The 12 slots reserved per weekday are drawn from
existing capacity.

See draft.md (Northside Community Clinic — Urgent Pediatric Same-Day
Appointment Notice) for the public-facing notice.
