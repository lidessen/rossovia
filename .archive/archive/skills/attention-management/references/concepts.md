# Attention Management Concepts

Load this reference when the live failure is difficult to distinguish from
context delivery, planning, memory, or task switching.

## Active attention

Active attention is the decision-relevant subset of available context, not an
observable store of Transformer attention weights. The distinction matters:
information can be present and retrievable yet fail to shape the next action.
[Lost in the Middle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172) demonstrates position
effects, while [RULER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654) shows that simple
retrieval success does not establish effective use of long context.

## Execution anchor

An execution anchor is a short, reconstructable projection:

```text
active goal + acceptance + authority/source + current phase
+ governing relation + retained obligations
```

It answers what the next action must remain about. It is not a project plan,
task database, memory canon, or authorization token. Replace it after a true
task switch or correction; do not accumulate several competing anchors.

## Governing relation

The governing relation is the current relation whose correct handling changes
the meaning of the next action: for example source/projection, Main/worker,
proposal/acceptance, local fix/whole outcome, or old assumption/new evidence.
It must arise from the actual object. A named Principle or role that does not
change the explanation is decorative.

## Cue, reset, and reform

- A **cue** makes an unchanged anchor salient again.
- A **reset** records a rejected assumption and replacement invariant, then
  replaces the obsolete attention unit.
- A **reform** reconstructs the anchor because the active task, phase, or
  authority actually changed.

The controls are not intensity levels. Repeating a stronger cue cannot repair
an obsolete anchor.

## Retained relation

A branch remains active only when it blocks current acceptance, invalidates a
load-bearing assumption, or changes the governing relation. Record the return
or reopen signal. A discovered defect is not automatically load-bearing; a
locally solved issue is not automatically the next mainline.

## Working disposition

A working disposition is a candidate prompt projection of accepted principles,
responsibilities, tensions, and habitual moves. It aims to bias decisions, not
to manufacture a human personality. It remains downstream of semantic sources
and must not invent priorities, authority, or facts.

Behavioral role evidence is mixed: a strategically constructed treatment
improved several reasoning tasks in
[Kong et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.228/), while a much larger
persona comparison found
[no overall factual benefit](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.888/).
Treat the carrier as versioned and replaceable.

## Habit cue and habit action

A habit cue is a recurring phrase. A habit action performs a compact cognitive
transition at a defined boundary. They can be combined, but must be evaluated
separately. Long-context instruction research found that prior enacted
compliance can affect later compliance more than instruction position alone
([Robinette et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-eacl.254/)); this does
not establish complex decision stability or persistent neural activation.

Prefer event boundaries over arbitrary token clocks: correction, phase change,
worker return, failed verification, branch settlement, compaction, or resume.
A recent long-horizon preprint found selective grounded reminders better than
always-on exposure in its tested Agents
([Remember When It Matters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08716)); retain the
preprint and task boundary when citing it.
