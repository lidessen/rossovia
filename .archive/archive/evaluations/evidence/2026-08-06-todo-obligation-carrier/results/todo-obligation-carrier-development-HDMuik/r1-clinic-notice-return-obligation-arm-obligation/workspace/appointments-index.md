# Urgent pediatric appointment routing

Caregivers request a same-day urgent pediatric slot by calling the clinic. No
app or online account is required.

A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the clinic's existing
triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that decision.

The same-day program is a six-week trial. It does not add clinicians or
increase the clinic's total appointment capacity, and it does not guarantee an
appointment.

Unused reserved slots are released to online booking at 14:00 for next-day
appointments.
