# Urgent pediatric appointment routing

- Request path: A caregiver requests a same-day slot by calling the clinic. No
  app or online account is required.
- Triage authority: A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the
  clinic's existing triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that decision.
- Trial boundary: The change is a six-week trial that measures pediatric waiting
  time and abandoned calls.
- Capacity boundary: The trial does not add clinicians and does not increase the
  clinic's total appointment capacity.
