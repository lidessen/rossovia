# Urgent pediatric appointment routing

## Request path

Caregivers request an urgent same-day appointment by calling the clinic. No app
or online account is required.

## Triage authority

A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the clinic's existing
triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that decision.

## Trial boundary

Starting August 5, 12 same-day slots are reserved each weekday for urgent
pediatric cases. This is a six-week trial; it will not necessarily continue
after six weeks.

## Capacity boundary

The trial does not add clinicians and does not increase the clinic's total
appointment capacity. Unused reserved slots are released to online booking at
14:00 for next-day appointments.
