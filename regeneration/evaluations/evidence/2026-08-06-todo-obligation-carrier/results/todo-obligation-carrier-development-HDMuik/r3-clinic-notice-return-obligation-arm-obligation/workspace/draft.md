# Northside Community Clinic — Urgent Pediatric Same-Day Appointment Notice

Starting **August 5**, Northside Community Clinic will reserve 12 same-day
appointment slots each weekday for urgent pediatric cases.

## How to request a slot

A caregiver may request a same-day slot by calling the clinic. No app or online
account is required.

A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent, using the clinic's existing
triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that decision.

## Schedule details

- Reserved slots not used that day are released to online booking at **14:00**
  for next-day appointments.
- The change is a **six-week trial**.
- During the trial, the clinic will measure pediatric waiting time and
  abandoned calls.
- The trial does not add clinicians and does not increase the clinic's total
  appointment capacity.

## Background

In the previous four weeks, 18 caregivers who called about a sick child were
told that no same-day appointment was available.

Please note that reserving these slots does not guarantee that an appointment
will be available. The trial's effects have not been determined, and the trial
is not scheduled to continue beyond the six-week period.
