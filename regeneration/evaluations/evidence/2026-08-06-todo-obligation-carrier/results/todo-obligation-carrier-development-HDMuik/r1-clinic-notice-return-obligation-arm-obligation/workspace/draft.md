# Clinic notice: same-day appointments for urgent pediatric cases

Northside Community Clinic will reserve 12 same-day appointment slots each
weekday for urgent pediatric cases, starting August 5.

## How to request a slot

A caregiver may request a same-day slot by calling the clinic. No app or online
account is required.

A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the clinic's existing
triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that decision.

## Trial details

The change is a six-week trial. During the trial, the clinic will measure
pediatric waiting time and abandoned calls.

Reserved slots not used that day are released to online booking at 14:00 for
next-day appointments.

The trial does not add clinicians or increase the clinic's total appointment
capacity. It does not guarantee an appointment for every urgent case.

## Background

In the previous four weeks, 18 caregivers who called for a sick child were told
that no same-day appointment was available.
