direction, production asset, or visual canon

**Source:** nine image-generation outputs produced during a Rossovia App logo
exploration and moved from the disposable `output/imagegen/` carrier on

**Use:** compare character, line, color, and app-icon treatments when a future

## Authority boundary

These images retain alternatives the Principal considered worth keeping. They
do not establish that Rossovia should use a female-presenting character, manga
or Japanese-comics vocabulary, any included color treatment, or any one image
as the public mark. A later production case must name its audience, surface,
accessibility constraints, inherited identity, and review question before
selecting or revising an asset.

The original generation prompts and model/session metadata were not retained
with the exported PNGs. The filenames are therefore working provenance labels,
not verified statements about style lineage. Do not attribute an image to an
artist, franchise, or protected character, and do not treat “researched” in a
filename as evidence of license clearance.
