# P04 — Interpretation

**Sequence source:** P04｜主要矛盾｜矛盾论

> Read P04 in `principles/SEQUENCE.md` first.

## Shared reading

Form the actual object and its relations before naming the contradiction that
organizes them. In that field of real tensions, find the one whose resolution
changes the shape of the others. It is not simply the loudest, most urgent, or
easiest problem. A principal contradiction is a leverage diagnosis and can
change as work proceeds.

## Decision questions

- If this judgment is wrong while all downstream work is right, does the work hold?
- Which tension, once resolved, changes the available actions for the others?
- Did the diagnosis arise from the object's relations, or from the vocabulary of
  a principle selected in advance?

## Boundary and common misreading

Do not derive the object from a preferred principle and then call the result its
principal contradiction. Do not use “principal” to erase secondary constraints.
P05 tests the concrete case and P15 preserves hard constraints while acting on
the diagnosis.

## Theoretical roots

- 毛泽东，[《矛盾论》](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-1/mswv1_17.htm)
  — principal and secondary contradictions must be investigated within the
  concrete relations and development of the thing.

## Expression probes

- A skill forms its live action gap and governing relation, then selects the
  current lead principle before composing supporting views.
- An architecture task locks the ownership/commit boundary before local cleanup.

## Revision discipline

Revise from a failed expression probe, counterexample, or corrected source root.
Clarify how to diagnose leverage. A new action-selection criterion belongs to
P15 or a candidate.
