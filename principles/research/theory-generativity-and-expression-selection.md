# Research — Theory Generativity and Expression Selection

**Disposition:** no-proposal
**Scope:** Determine why the current Principle Sequence can constrain safe work
yet still fail to generate the object-level correction later supplied by the
Principal, and whether the remedy requires a new principle, a new runtime
mechanism, or a repair to existing expression.
**Source limitations:** The comparison uses two internal historical cases and
matched Agent probes in one repository. It tests decision formation, not
cross-model reliability, and does not establish that every skill or Work Cell
activation has the same failure.

> This record is cited, revisable research. It owns no P-ID, cannot redefine an
> interpretation, and does not propose a Sequence change by itself.

## Question

Has the system weakened the movement from theory to concrete action by using
Principles mainly as downstream constraints, labels, and acceptance boundaries?
If so, can the current Sequence recover its generative role without adding a
new schema, preflight, gate, or standing committee?

## Historical comparisons

The first blind comparison used the bounded structural evidence retained in the
[sparse multihead code-refactor probe](../../regeneration/evaluations/2026-07-14-code-refactor-routing-probe.md);
the exact fixture identity, matched conditions, and retained outputs are in the
[blind-probe evidence record](evidence/theory-generativity-blind-probes-2026-07-26.md#code-refactor-case).
One Agent fixed its P-ID team from task intent before inspecting the object; a
matched Agent formed the object before selecting the team. Both independently
recommended the same transition: preserve the runtime, add only a bounded
evaluation, and do not execute the proposed refactor. This case did not support
changing the Sequence runtime order.

The second comparison stopped the naming experiment immediately before the
historical [human rejection of the archive surface](../../regeneration/evaluations/2026-07-13-activation-field-scale-probe.md#human-rejection-of-the-archive-surface);
its cutoff, fixture digest, protocol, and retained outputs are in the
[naming-surface evidence](evidence/theory-generativity-blind-probes-2026-07-26.md#naming-surface-case).
The fixture retained the actual candidate surfaces, treatment costs, shared
semantic basin, archive-selection qualities, intended audience, and missing
direct baseline, but withheld the later correction.

The intent-first and object-first Agents again converged. Both selected P15 as
lead with authority or fact-admission supports and recommended adding a direct
baseline before asking the Principal to compare treatments. That was a valid
experimental correction, but neither independently identified the deeper
historical failure: both arms rewarded estrangement, exposed unclassified
associative intermediates as if they were decision-ready names, and therefore
offered two variants of one distorted surface.

Three separately constrained readings then tested capacity rather than default
selection; their retained outputs and limits are recorded in the

| Reading | Decision-changing result |
|---|---|
| P06 — essence and phenomenon | Distinguished title coverage from creative quality, but still stopped at adding the missing direct baseline. |
| P07 — concrete, abstraction, reconstructed concrete | Identified the two arms as samples from one generative world and the packet as a premature reconstruction of intermediate material into a naming decision. |
| P16 — form enables action | Identified the category error between internal associative material and the concrete artifact the Principal could judge, including the shared risk of amplifying unfamiliarity. |

The later experiment record independently contains the same two corrections:
the packet lacked a plain direct baseline, and candidate-field artifacts were
not yet audience-ready expressions. Its subsequent
[expression projection and surface-gate probes](../../regeneration/evaluations/2026-07-13-activation-field-scale-probe.md#expression-projection-and-surface-gate-probes)
showed that a plainness instruction alone merely changed maximal distance into
generic craft language. The historical evidence therefore supports a relation
among object formation, generative structure, and expression form—not another
style rule.

## Diagnosis

The current Sequence contains enough theory to produce the stronger correction,
but default expression selection did not activate it. The repeated P15/P13/P11
team optimized a downstream question—what is the smallest defensible next
action and who may accept it—before explaining what kind of object the
experiment had actually produced.

This is not evidence that P15, P13, or P11 are wrong. It is evidence of an
ordering inversion:

1. a task asks for a recommendation;
2. selection chooses an action-limiting or authority principle;
3. that vocabulary supplies the principal contradiction;
4. the concrete object is then read mainly to populate the chosen boundary.

The intended movement is the reverse:

1. form the actual object, subject, history, and governing relations;
2. diagnose the contradiction that organizes them;
3. select the Principle that changes the explanation or transformation;
4. apply P15, authority, verification, and projection boundaries where the
   resulting action actually needs them.

## Existing-sequence coverage

- **P04** already requires a principal contradiction to be diagnosed within a
  field of real tensions. Its expression example was ambiguous enough to sound
  like Principle selection preceded object formation.
- **P07** already owns the movement from actual concrete through necessary
  determinations to a reconstructed concrete whole. It explains why measurable
  divergence is not yet a decision-ready naming object.
- **P15** already begins “after identifying the present contradiction,” but its
  short Sequence line makes it an attractive generic lead whenever a task asks
  for a next step.
- **P16** already requires the form to enable the actual subject's action. It
  explains why an archive surface and a candidate name are different artifacts.

No missing cross-context principle was observed. The failure is expression and
selection drift within the existing lineage.

## Applied repair

The smallest repair changes no Sequence line and adds no runtime stage:

- clarify P04 so object relations precede contradiction and lead selection;
- clarify P15 as downstream action selection rather than a universal task lead;
- add P07 and P16 expression probes for intermediate generative material versus
  a concrete decision object;
- require skill expression teams to survive in the core method rather than
  remain lineage labels; and
- revise the existing Sequence selector instruction so the contradiction comes
  from the task's concrete object, the lead changes its explanation or
  transformation, and supports are not filled only with downstream safeguards.

These are changes to existing semantic and prompt surfaces. They do not add a
formation schema, admission call, fixed P-ID committee, read tool, acceptance
gate, or orchestration branch.

## Post-repair replay

Two fresh Agents received the same hidden historical cutoff and the revised
selector wording. The [retained replay evidence](evidence/theory-generativity-blind-probes-2026-07-26.md#post-repair-replays)
records that their lead changed from P15 to P04. They explicitly treated the
two arms as products of one shared model, archive, and semantic basin rather
than as independent evidence, and refused to send the current packet. This is a
real decision-path improvement over choosing the minimum next action first.

The replays did not fully recover the later Principal correction. Their supports
remained P08, P11, and P13, and they still proposed converting one existing arm
into a comparison against a direct baseline rather than first reconstructing
the intermediate material into an audience-ready naming object. The result is
therefore directional evidence, not proof that a generic selector can replace a
domain method.

The existing
[`naming-and-articulation` lineage and core method](../../skills/naming-and-articulation/SKILL.md#core-method)
already supply the missing composition: P06/P07/P16/P15, recover the relation
before the word, generate from that relation, and test the actual actor's
decision. Do not duplicate that method in the generic Sequence runtime merely
to make this fixture pass.

## Disconfirming evidence

Reopen the diagnosis if matched historical or live probes show any of the
following:

- the revised selector still defaults to a downstream constraint while missing
  an available object-forming principle;
- object-level corrections improve only when a fixed principle team is named in
  advance;
- the extra selector instruction reduces correct bounded decisions by forcing
  unnecessary abstraction; or
- a recurrent decision-changing remainder cannot be reduced to P04, P07, P15,
  P16, or another current Sequence line.

Until then, do not propose a new P-ID or promote the comparison harness into a
permanent mechanism.

## 2026-08-03 top-down review

The later architecture-and-Skills review did not produce evidence for adding,
revising, or retiring a Sequence line. P01–P16 still cover the observed
decision-changing failures. The strongest generic selector repair remains
directional rather than universal: object-first replays improved the decision
path, but domain methods were still needed to reconstruct the full object.

The review did expose four downstream expression failures:

- project purpose and core method had been described as if one semantic source
  owned both; the Founding Mandate now owns purpose and value while the
  Sequence remains the sole semantic root of core principles;
- volatile Workbench command and authorization protocols had accumulated in the
  repository-wide Agent entry, making an upper context carrier change at the
  speed of a lower mechanism;
- repeated recovery and authorization fixes showed that prior validation is
  evidence rather than present effect authority after source, target, or
  authorization drift; P13 and the accepted architecture now state this
  consequence without adding a new P-ID; and
- P14's Sequence-to-interpretation expression probe exceeded P14's
  reconstructibility predicate. It has been replaced with a source/projection
  case; semantic-source authority remains an architecture relation.

These findings reinforce the existing disposition: repair ownership, context
delivery, interpretation provenance, and behavior evidence before changing the
Sequence. Reopen the no-proposal conclusion only if the repaired layers still
produce a recurring cross-context decision failure that no current P-ID can
bear without semantic extension.
