# Experimental Disposition Carriers

Load this reference only when constructing or comparing a prompt-conditioned
working disposition. These carriers are hypotheses, not the core method.

The owning hypothesis history is the repository research record
`principles/research/agent-attention-disposition-and-habit.md`. An installed
Skill can use this method without treating that host path as a source.

## Semantic-atom gate

Before writing prose, extract accepted source atoms:

| Atom | Source | Decision consequence | Tension or exception |
|---|---|---|---|
| position | who the Agent serves and what it owns | action it may initiate | authority it cannot claim |
| object | what whole the Agent must preserve | what it notices first | local detail that must stay secondary |
| relation | source/projection, Main/worker, proposal/acceptance, or another live relation | preferred transition | reopening condition |
| habit | repeated decision move | trigger boundary | case where the habit must not fire |

Reject the composition if an atom has no accepted source or if prose adds a new
priority, duty, power, or factual claim.

## Carrier forms

Compare, do not assume, these forms:

1. **Role label:** a short identity noun. It is cheap and often decorative.
2. **Relation kernel:** direct responsibilities and boundaries without a
   personality narrative.
3. **Principle list:** the same semantic atoms as separate statements.
4. **Integrated character:** the same atoms composed into a connected account
   of position, responsibility, tension, and habitual response.
5. **Role-entry pass:** before consequential action, reconstruct position,
   object, governing relation, acceptance, and likely distraction without
   solving the task yet.
6. **Catchphrase:** a short recurring phrase which compresses one governing
   relation.
7. **Habit action:** at a material boundary, reconstruct the object, relation,
   present contradiction, and next action.

Keep semantic atoms and token budgets comparable when attributing an effect.
Do not add flattering expertise, biography, names, colors, or fictional history
unless those are the variable under test.

## Integrated-character candidate

Write connected prose rather than repeating P-IDs. A candidate may express:

- the Agent's responsibility to the human Principal;
- the whole it keeps in view;
- what it habitually notices before acting;
- how it treats evidence, authority, workers, and acceptance;
- which tension changes its next move;
- how it responds when evidence or the Principal rejects an assumption.

Do not make it universally admirable. A useful carrier is concrete enough to
produce different action under tension and short enough not to compete with the
task.

Record it as:

```text
Carrier ID and revision:
Accepted source atoms:
Expected decision tendency:
Prompt treatment:
Information lost or implication added:
Trigger and lifetime:
Disconfirming observation:
Standing: unverified | supported locally | rejected | inconclusive
Evidence links:
```

## Role-entry candidate

Bound the orientation pass:

```text
Before acting, do not solve the task yet.
Reconstruct your position, the actual object, its governing relations,
the current acceptance condition, and the distraction most likely to pull the
next action away from them. Then rebind the already chosen candidate action, or
hand off when selecting the next practice remains unresolved.
```

Compare this with an equal-cost non-role abstraction or observation pass. If
extra reasoning time explains the result, the whole treatment may still be
useful, but “entering character” remains unproven.

## Catchphrase and habit candidates

A catchphrase must compress a real relation rather than advertise identity.
Examples such as “the branch serves the whole” or “claims return through
evidence” are treatments, not doctrine. Preserve the exact wording and source
atom, then test it against equal-token neutral recurrence.

A habit action should be event-triggered and externally inspectable. Example:

```text
Boundary:
Current object and governing relation:
Disturbance: continue | retain | return | switch | ask
Rebound candidate action or handoff:
```

Do not require the visible packet on every turn. The action matters only when
it changes or verifies the next decision.
