# Clinic notice: same-day urgent pediatric appointments

Northside Community Clinic is updating how urgent pediatric same-day
appointments are handled.

## What changes on August 5

Starting August 5, the clinic will reserve 12 same-day appointment slots each
weekday for urgent pediatric cases. This is a six-week trial.

## How to request a same-day slot

A caregiver may request a same-day slot by calling the clinic. No app or online
account is required.

A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the clinic's existing
triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that decision.

## About the trial

Reserved slots not used that day are released to online booking at 14:00 for
next-day appointments.

During the trial, the clinic will measure pediatric waiting time and abandoned
calls.

The trial does not add clinicians and does not increase the clinic's total
appointment capacity.

The trial does not guarantee an appointment. Its effects on emergency-room use
and outcomes are not part of this notice, and the trial will not necessarily
continue after six weeks.

## Background

In the previous four weeks, 18 caregivers who called for a sick child were told
that no same-day appointment was available. That observation is provided for
context only.
