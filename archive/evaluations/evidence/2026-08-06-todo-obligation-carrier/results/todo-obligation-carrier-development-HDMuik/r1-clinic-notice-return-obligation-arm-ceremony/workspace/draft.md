# Northside Community Clinic — Same-day pediatric appointments

Starting August 5, Northside Community Clinic will reserve 12 same-day
appointment slots each weekday for urgent pediatric cases.

## How to request a same-day slot

A caregiver may request a same-day slot by calling the clinic. No app or online
account is required. A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the
clinic's existing triage protocol; front-desk staff do not make that decision.

## What to expect

- Reserved slots not used that day are released to online booking at 14:00 for
  next-day appointments.
- The change is a six-week trial.
- During the trial, the clinic will measure pediatric waiting time and
  abandoned calls.
- The trial does not add clinicians and does not increase the clinic's total
  appointment capacity.

## Background

In the previous four weeks, 18 caregivers who called for a sick child were told
that no same-day appointment was available.
