# Urgent pediatric appointment routing

- Request path: A caregiver requests a same-day slot by calling the clinic. No
  app or online account is required.
- Triage authority: A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the
  clinic's existing triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that
  decision.
- Trial boundary: The same-day reservation program is a six-week trial. It is
  not guaranteed to continue after six weeks.
- Capacity boundary: The trial does not add clinicians and does not increase
  the clinic's total appointment capacity.

Unused reserved slots are released to online booking at 14:00 for next-day
appointments.
