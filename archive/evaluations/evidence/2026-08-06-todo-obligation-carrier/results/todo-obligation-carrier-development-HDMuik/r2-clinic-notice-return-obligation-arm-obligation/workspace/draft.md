# Notice: Same-Day Urgent Pediatric Appointment Slots

Starting August 5, Northside Community Clinic will reserve 12 same-day
appointment slots each weekday for urgent pediatric cases. This reservation is
a six-week trial.

## How to request a slot

A caregiver may request a same-day slot by calling the clinic. No app or online
account is required.

## Who decides whether a case is urgent

A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the clinic's existing
triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that decision.

## Unused slots

Reserved slots not used that day are released to online booking at 14:00 for
next-day appointments.

## What the trial measures

During the six-week trial, the clinic will measure pediatric waiting time and
abandoned calls.

## Scope of the trial

The trial does not add clinicians and does not increase the clinic's total
appointment capacity.

## Background

In the previous four weeks, 18 caregivers who called for a sick child were told
that no same-day appointment was available.
