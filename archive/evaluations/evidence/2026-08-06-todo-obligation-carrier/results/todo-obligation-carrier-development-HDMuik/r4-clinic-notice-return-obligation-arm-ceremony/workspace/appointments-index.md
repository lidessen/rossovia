# Urgent pediatric appointment routing

## Request path

Starting August 5, a caregiver requests a same-day urgent pediatric slot by
calling the clinic. No app or online account is required.

## Triage authority

A triage nurse decides whether a case is urgent under the clinic's existing
triage protocol. Front-desk staff do not make that decision.

## Trial boundary

This change is a six-week trial. Each weekday the clinic reserves 12 same-day
appointment slots for urgent pediatric cases. Reserved slots not used that day
are released to online booking at 14:00 for next-day appointments. The trial
does not add clinicians.

## Capacity boundary

The trial does not increase the clinic's total appointment capacity. A same-day
slot is not guaranteed.
