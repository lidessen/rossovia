# Exciting Changes to Pediatric Care

Northside Community Clinic is thrilled to announce an innovative new approach that puts families first. Starting August 5, we will reserve 12 same-day appointment slots each weekday for urgent pediatric cases.

## Key Benefits

- Fast and seamless access
- A family-centered experience
- Flexible online options

Caregivers can call the clinic to request a slot. A triage nurse uses the existing triage protocol to decide whether a case is urgent. Front-desk staff do not make this decision.
